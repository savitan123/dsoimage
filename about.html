About Shimon Avitan

Deep Sky Astrophotographer · Israel

Who I Am
I’m Shimon Avitan, an amateur astronomer and deep-sky astrophotographer based in Israel. This site is a curated archive of my imaging work — galaxies, nebulae, and star clusters captured under the dark skies of the Negev Desert and the Golan Heights. My goal is to present each target with honest detail: real structure, true color, and no over-processed artifice. Every preview links to a full-resolution frame with exposure notes and catalog data.
